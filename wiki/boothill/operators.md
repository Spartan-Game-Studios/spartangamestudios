# Operators

Boothill is played as an **operator** — a character with their own weapon feel, starting kit, and a stat sheet that records every run.

Each operator tracks its own best survival time, kills, wins, and run count, and those per-operator bests are what the leaderboard ranks.

If you know an operator's kit well, a pull request filling in their details is welcome — one section per operator.
